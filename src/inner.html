Getting paid

What gets approved

Rejected + a strike

  • Under 15 seconds, or the length is faked (2 strikes)
  • The sound does not start in the right place (0:01 on TikTok, 2:01 on Instagram)
  • The song stops less than 12 seconds after the drop
  • Not the official sound, or the song is not clearly audible
  • Caption missing the credit, the hashtag or the artist tag, or no link in bio
  • Not vertical, watermarked, or a photo slideshow
  • You used an edit, text or caption claimed by someone else

Rejected, no strike

  • The song was closed when you posted
  • Likes under 1% of views
  • Audience not majority Tier 1

Strikes last 90 days. 1: retake the course. 2: 30 days off music campaigns. 3: removed.

Clip of the Week: $30 for the best music edit each week.

At 1,000 views: send a screen recording of that video's analytics.

Where the time comes from

The TikTok sound is song 2:00 to 3:00 and the drop sits at 2:04, measured against the full track. START is the drop minus 3 seconds, rounded down: song 2:01. TikTok numbers that same instant 0:01 because its sound begins at 2:00 (2:01 minus 2:00). Instagram uses the full song, so it keeps 2:01. TikTok moved this sound on September 15 (it used to begin at 1:42), which is why the generator now re-checks live sounds every day.

The download

WAV, 48 kHz, cut from the official upload with no re-encoding. If the label sends a master, replace the file: clippers should never edit on a low quality MP3.

Caps

Counted = approved clips matched to this song by sound id. At the cap the song closes, and clips posted later are rejected with K1 (no strike).

Requirements · ENU (AU) · I Believe in Love
SONG-FIRST RULES (MUSIC CAMPAIGN)
Break any of rules 1 to 7 and your submission is rejected, earns $0, and counts as a strike.

1. LENGTH: 15 seconds minimum, 60 seconds max. The song plays the whole time. No looping one shot, no freeze frames, no black screens.

2. START THE SOUND IN THE RIGHT PLACE, so the drop lands about 3 seconds into your video. TikTok numbers the sound on its own timeline and Instagram uses the full song, so use the line for the app you are posting on.
TikTok: start the sound at 0:01 - https://www.tiktok.com/music/I-Believe-in-Love-7675063812504569873
Instagram: start the song at 2:01 - https://www.instagram.com/reels/audio/1393563806032853

3. 12 SECONDS AFTER THE DROP: keep the song playing to at least 2:16.

4. OFFICIAL SOUND ONLY: full volume. No original sound, sped up, slowed, remixed or muted versions. No dialogue over the song.

5. CAPTION: your caption includes 🎵 I Believe in Love - ENU (AU), the hashtag #ibelieveinlove and a tag of @enu.music. Keep [SMART LINK] in your bio until [END DATE].

6. FORMAT: vertical 9:16. No watermarks. English on-screen text and captions. No photo slideshows.

7. CLAIM WHAT YOU USE: pre-made edits, on-screen texts and captions are claimed on the campaign page: [GUIDELINES LINK]. Claim before you post and add your post link within 48 hours. Using an item claimed by another clipper is rejected.

8. SONG CAPS: each song has a view cap shown on the campaign page. Clips posted after a song is marked Closed are rejected (no strike).

9. ANALYTICS: once your video passes 1,000 views, send a screen recording of that video's analytics (watch time, view graphs, audience locations). Likes must be at least 1% of views, and your audience must be majority Tier 1.

10. ACCOUNTS: no botting, story boosting, campaign-only accounts, collab posts, or posting the same clip on more than one account. Likes and comments stay on.

11. STRIKES (they count for 90 days): strike 1 = retake Music Clipping 101 before your next music submission. Strike 2 = suspended from all music campaigns for 30 days. Strike 3 = removed from music campaigns. Faking length counts as 2 strikes. Botting, faked analytics, reposting someone else's clip, or deleting, hiding or changing the sound on an approved clip = removed immediately.

  4. Add 2 to 3 reference clips per edit type.
